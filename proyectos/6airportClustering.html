European Airport Traffic Classification

  • Python, Pandas
  • Data Mining
  • 2026
  • ES, EN

The problem

European airports serve vastly different functions, some are international hubs, others are regional or leisure-focused, yet air navigation providers often apply the same infrastructure and investment strategies to all of them. The challenge was to identify meaningful patterns in airport traffic data that would reveal each airport's true profile: its typical volume, weekly rhythm and seasonal patterns. That classification could then drive differentiated investment priorities.

What I built

A complete KDD (Knowledge Discovery in Databases) pipeline applied to EUROCONTROL's official Airport Traffic dataset. The process spanned selection of data directly from the official source, exploratory analysis, structural data quality assessment and feature engineering at the airport level.

The second phase focused on data preparation: I recreated temporal variables (day of week, month), aggregated daily records to airport-level summaries and engineered traffic profile variables: total volume, average daily traffic, weekday to weekend ratios and summer to rest of year ratios. These features capture the distinct signature of each airport's traffic pattern and form the foundation for classification.

The analysis also uncovered macro patterns: Spain's leading position in total traffic volume despite no single airport matching Paris-CDG or Amsterdam, driven by multiple medium-sized hubs plus extensive leisure-airport coverage; and the sharp traffic collapse in March 2020 with gradual recovery through 2024.

Outcome

A clean, well-engineered dataset ready for classification algorithms, with features that distinguish between hub, regional, business and leisure airports. The work revealed that airport role cannot be inferred from volume alone, weekly and seasonal patterns are equally telling. Stakeholders now have actionable insights: ANS providers and airport managers can differentiate infrastructure investment based on traffic profile rather than size and can anticipate demand patterns specific to each airport type.
