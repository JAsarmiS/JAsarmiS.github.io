About

// profile

Computer Engineering graduate, currently completing a Master's in Big Data & Data Science while working two part-time jobs in Switzerland. I've never been drawn to the data itself, what actually gets me interested is what's underneath it: the patterns, the "why", and what it can be used for. I'd rather spend longer understanding why something works than settle for the first answer that does.

I moved to Switzerland a year ago without speaking a word of German, and today I work customer-facing in it, probably the clearest proof I have that starting from zero doesn't scare me if it's worth it. Outside work and study, I follow aviation, geography and geopolitics closely, and I'm usually the one who asks one question too many until something actually clicks.
